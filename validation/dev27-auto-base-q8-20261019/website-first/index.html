Welcome

Whether you have a balcony, a backyard, or simply a love of plants, Riverside Garden Club is a friendly place to learn, share, and grow. Our members maintain shared beds, exchange seedlings, host practical workshops, and bring color to public spaces.

  • Open monthly meetings with short talks and open discussion.
  • Community plots available for members who want hands-on growing space.
  • Tool library, seed exchange, and seasonal plant swaps.
