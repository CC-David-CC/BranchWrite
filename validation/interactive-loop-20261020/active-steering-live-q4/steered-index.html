Care basics

Daily needs
  • Fresh water and a consistent feeding routine.
  • Walks, sniff time, and safe play every day.
  • A comfortable resting spot away from drafts and noise.
  • Basic grooming: brushing, nail checks, and ear checks.
Training
  • Use short, positive sessions with treats or praise.
  • Teach cues like come, sit, stay, and leave it.
  • Be patient with house training and socialization.
  • Keep routines predictable to reduce stress.
Health
  • Schedule regular veterinary checkups and vaccinations.
  • Watch appetite, energy, breathing, and stool changes.
  • Keep a safe chew and toy rotation to prevent boredom.
  • Ask a veterinarian about spay/neuter and parasite prevention.
