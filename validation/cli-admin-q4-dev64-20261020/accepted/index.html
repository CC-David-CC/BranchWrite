Dogs: Friendly Companions

Dogs are loyal, social animals that have lived alongside people for thousands of years. They come in many sizes, coats, and temperaments.

Why people love dogs

  • Companionship and play
  • Exercise and routine
  • Service, therapy, and working roles

Basic care

  • Fresh water and balanced food
  • Daily walks and mental enrichment
  • Veterinary checkups and training

Adoption note

Consider adopting from a shelter or rescue. Many dogs need homes and can thrive with patient, responsible care.
