The model can think before its constrained final answer. Different sampler
settings can explore different continuations from a saved thought. This first
version uses host-registered0/128 budgets through existing grammar-state controls;
a general per-generation thinking_length option is still missing.

For128 arms, a real CLI call consumed four thinking tokens, published the tip,
forked it, then resumed greedy and sampled siblings. Sampled settings were
temperature0.7, Min-P0.05, Top-K60 and Top-P0.95. This does not isolate Min-P.
The zero-budget short arm paused after its label but before EOS; its sampled
sibling therefore only sampled the remaining end token, not a new thought.
All continuations finished with consumed EOS, legal labels, unchanged saved
root bytes and unchanged partial controllers. Real forward deltas equal actual
generated-token counts. No saved prefix replay was charged in those deltas.

Both short-case thoughts actually describe the square as upper-left. Greedy's
NOT_VISIBLE contradicts its text. Both128-token thoughts were cut mid-sentence.
Final legal-label scores were not captured, so cause is unresolved. The next
diagnostic is longer budgets/multiple seeds plus final-score inspection. A model
thought is output evidence, not direct proof of its internal visual computation.

The matcher uses one canonical controller and accepted history. Its budget is
part of the immutable registry specification; replay reconstructs phase/counts.
The existing sampler retains RNG and other progress through closure. This model's
verified closing marker is dedicated user-defined GGUF type4 token248069, not EOS.
Ordinary-text delimiter tokenizations remain unsupported. Final GBNF/JSON
constraints and tool authority remain host-controlled. Client owns logical tips;
COW owns RAM/disk/GPU placement and expiry.
